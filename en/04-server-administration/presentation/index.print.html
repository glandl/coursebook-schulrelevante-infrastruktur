Role presentation

Required, not graded

The role presentation is required but not graded. Its purpose is to show the others what you built. Only the role runbook is graded.

When: 23.11.2026, 11:30–12:30 · Duration: 6 minutes per team, then changeover · Audience: all other teams, who did not build your role

What goes into the six minutes

MinuteContent
1The role at a school in two sentences, with an example from everyday school life
2–4Live or as screenshots: an acceptance test in the Windows world and the same test in the Linux world, from the test client
5One combination of Windows and Linux that worked (or why not)
6Comparison and recommendation: one slide or table with effort, costs, vendor dependence. Which world would you recommend to Mittelschule Musterstadt?

Tips

  • Live demos in the Proxmox lab are welcome. Still keep screenshots ready in case something fails at exactly that moment.
  • Whoever is not speaking operates the console. How you split up is your team’s decision.
  • Do not show long lists of commands; those are in the runbook. Show the result.
  • One slide is enough. Anything that can be shown in the classroom is allowed, including your README in the browser.

For the audience

Note one question or observation per role. At the end we collect together: where did Linux have the advantage, where Windows, and where did it not matter? The result is the starting point for session 5 (device management) and for the architecture decision in the capstone project.
