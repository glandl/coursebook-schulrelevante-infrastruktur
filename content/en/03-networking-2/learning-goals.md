After this chapter you can:

1. explain **why** a school network is divided into VLANs (separating groups, smaller broadcast domains, flexibility without additional hardware),
2. describe the difference between an **access port** and a **trunk port** and explain what the **802.1Q tag** in an Ethernet frame contains,
3. justify why devices in different VLANs can only talk to each other via a **router** (or layer-3 switch) and why permission or denial is decided **there**: a VLAN separates, an access rule permits,
4. read a **permission matrix** (who may reach which service in which network) and translate it into access rules,
5. explain the **DHCP** sequence (Discover, Offer, Request, Acknowledge), the most important **DHCP options** and the job of a **DHCP relay**,
6. describe the path of a **DNS query** from the device via the resolver to the responsible server and distinguish the record types A, AAAA, PTR, CNAME, MX and SRV,
7. use `dig`, `getent`, `/etc/resolv.conf` (Linux) as well as `ipconfig /all`, `nslookup` and `Resolve-DnsName` (Windows) to find out which address, which DNS server and which gateway a device uses,
8. explain how a **captive portal** in the guest Wi-Fi works.
